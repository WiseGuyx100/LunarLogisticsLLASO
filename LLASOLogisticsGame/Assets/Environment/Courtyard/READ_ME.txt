Earth Natural Landscaping

Import while GameTest is closed, then reopen Assets/Scenes/TestGame/GameTest.unity. This package updates that scene and replaces the prior Courtyard Landscaping root. Concrete excludes the warehouse floor footprint. 160 exterior shrubs are scattered in irregular groups, with varied spacing, rotation and height of 0.9-1.35 m. Eight planted beds sit along inside wall edges, keeping the centre cargo route clear. Grass remains outside the walls, with additional short grass in the raised beds. The downloaded Bermuda lawn meshes are 10-15 cm high, scattered without rows near the facility and in irregular clusters across the exterior terrain, and combined into spatial chunks. No scripts or editor tools are included. Existing terrain supplies collisions. The concrete surface is 0.015 m above flat terrain. Shared grass layers also affect other scenes using those layers.

Source models: Poly Haven shrub_02, shrub_sorrel_01, grass_bermuda_01, CC0. Original geometry and leaf UVs converted from the downloaded Blender files.
