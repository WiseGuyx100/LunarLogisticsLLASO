# Electric Tug — Futuristic Heavy-Cargo Edition

A cutting-edge, programmatically built heavy-duty electric cargo tug designed for futuristic logistics games. Features an expanded massive cargo bed with high safety containment walls, a sleek white-blue-purple-green cyber aesthetic, and unobstructed first-person driving views.

## Features
- **Massive Expanded Cargo Bed**: Lengthened (3.8m) and widened (1.85m) with high reinforced containment walls (1.0m) and solid physics colliders to securely hold large boxes without spilling or glitching.
- **Futuristic Cyber Color Palette**: Predominantly sleek futuristic white, accented with electric blue, deep cyber purple, and glowing neon green light strips.
- **Sci-Fi Aesthetic**: Aerodynamic low-profile nose, glowing neon LED strips, tinted sci-fi panoramic glass, and a top safety beacon.
- **Clear-View Driver Seating**: Optimized seat mount and hidden first-person avatar meshes ensure 100% clear sightlines through the panoramic windshield.
- **Heavy-Duty Physics**: 5,000 kg mass with low center of gravity, smooth acceleration, braking, and 4-zone approach entry.

## Setup Instructions
1. Copy the `ElectricTug_Futuristic_HeavyCargo` folder into your Unity project's `Assets/` directory.
2. Allow Unity to compile scripts.
3. Use **GameObject → Logistics Game → Create Futuristic Heavy Cargo Tug**.
4. Press **Play** and press **F** near any side of the tug to enter.
