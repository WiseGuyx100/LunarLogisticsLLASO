# CyberHex Gorilla Cart (Small)

Small, hand-pulled vehicle in the CyberHex family (large = Electric Tug, medium = Pack Mule).
Shape inspired by the Gorilla dump cart: four big wheels, open tub, long handle. Colours, hexagons and glow match the large tug.
The tub is see-through hex glass with glowing ribs and the handle ends in a glowing hex grip loop.

## Setup
1. Copy this folder into your project and let Unity compile.
2. Menu: **GameObject -> Logistics Game -> Create CyberHex Gorilla Cart (Small)**.
3. Meshes/materials/prefab are saved to `Assets/HexGorillaCart_Small/`.

## Controls
- **F** grab / release the handle (stand near the cart). While held, it trails behind you as you walk normally.
- **Z** dump: tub tilts and the glass gate swings open (while holding the handle or standing next to it).

## View
You keep your normal first-person view: the cart trails behind you and the handle is held beside/behind you, out of the camera's way.
Tune `handRight` / `handBack` on the controller if you want the handle somewhere else.
