# CyberHex Pack Mule (Medium)

Medium vehicle in the CyberHex family (large = Electric Tug, small = Gorilla Cart).
Shape inspired by the Pack Mule: low flat deck, tall control tower at the back, stand-on operator platform, twin front casters.
Colours, hexagons and glow match the large tug. The whole cargo tray is see-through hex glass.

## Setup
1. Copy this folder into your project and let Unity compile.
2. Menu: **GameObject -> Logistics Game -> Create CyberHex Pack Mule (Medium)**.
3. Meshes/materials/prefab are saved to `Assets/HexPackMule_Medium/`.

## Controls
- **F** step on / off the platform (stand next to the mule)
- **W / S** drive, **A / D** steer
- **Z** lower / raise the glass lift-gate ramp (while mounted or standing next to it). Roll boxes straight on/off.
  The ramp auto-closes if you drive away quickly.

## View
You stand on the rear platform. The tower is behind you, the yoke sits low in front of your waist, and the tray is
see-through glass, so you can see the ground ahead. Eye height assumes a standard 1.8 m capsule player;
move the child object **Player Stand Mount** up/down to adjust.
